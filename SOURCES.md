## Ukraine and Russia: bridges attacked

Ukraine attacked the Kerch crossing for a second consecutive day. Reuters' later
report describes the main road arch falling into the sea, based on verified
images. Russia attacked Kyiv's bridges and other infrastructure. The image
shows damage to the road crossing and does not imply destruction of the railway.

## Iran, Bahrain and the United States

Iran launched drones at a US radar installation in Bahrain. Bahrain and the US
said they intercepted the drones without reported damage or injuries to US
personnel. A US official said a fighter jet later struck an Iranian missile
launch site in the Strait of Hormuz. The image attributes the interception and
retaliation rather than portraying the Iranian claim of radar damage as fact.

- [Associated Press, 17 November](https://apnews.com/article/582e0b5d51be06f0ed630fac24f39b72)
- [Reuters, syndicated by ThePrint](https://theprint.in/world/iran-attacks-us-military-radar-system-in-bahrain-with-drones/3101071/)

## Sudan: new UN report on civilian deaths

The UN Human Rights Office released its report on 17 November. It confirmed
over 2,230 civilian deaths from January through October, including more than
1,630 killed by drones. The image labels these as deaths documented for
January–October, not deaths occurring on 17 November. The real total may be higher.

- [United Nations in Sudan](https://sudan.un.org/en/326318-un-report-details-deadly-civilian-impact-surge-drone-strikes)
- [UN Geneva briefing, 17 November](https://www.ungeneva.org/en/news-media/press-briefing/2026/11/un-geneva-press-briefing-2)

## Nvidia: quarterly earnings

Nvidia announced Q3 fiscal 2027 revenue of USD 115.8 billion, up 103% from a

These are reported revenues, not forecasts or a stock-price increase.

## Canada and China: tariff relief

Xi Jinping and Mark Carney met in Shenzhen on Tuesday and expanded tariff
relief. China offered relief on approximately CAD 400 million of Canadian
exports; Canada extended exemptions for certain Chinese steel and aluminium
products by two years. The image does not imply all tariffs were abolished.

- [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/apec-china-canada-xi-carney-1427deb4175e27c61efec6ee8eef36e8)

## Brazil: Awá communities face pressure

On Tuesday, Survival International released footage it said showed an Awá
encounter the previous day. Funai said it was assessing the footage and had
activated a health contingency plan; advocates warned of pressure from loggers
and ranchers. Reuters could not independently verify the footage's date and
location. The drawing represents a threatened forest, not the footage itself.

- [Reuters, syndicated by Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/rare-images-of-isolatedindigenous-group-in-brazil-highlight-threats-5034223)
